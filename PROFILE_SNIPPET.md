# GitHub and résumé copy

## Suggested repository description

E-commerce sales mix and data-quality diagnostic using Excel Data Model summaries, Python, and SQL. Includes transparent caveats and practical merchandising follow-up actions.

## Résumé bullet

Analyzed category and Top 10 city sales summaries from a multi-table Excel Data Model using Python and SQL; quantified category mix and attribution gaps, documented source limitations, and proposed validation and controlled-test steps before merchandising investment.

## Three-minute interview walkthrough

1. **Business question:** Where are reported sales concentrated, and can the numbers support a merchandising decision?
2. **Approach:** Reconciled the 11 category summaries, measured top-three share, examined only the displayed Top 10 cities, and flagged a blank customer bucket and category/product examples for validation.
3. **Recommendation:** Do not move spend based on revenue alone. Verify the measure, period, currency, customer keys, and product/category mapping; then add margin, returns, order count, trends, and stock availability.
4. **Next experiment:** Once those checks pass, run a controlled merchandising or market test and evaluate incremental contribution margin against a holdout.

## Be transparent in the interview

This is an aggregate diagnostic, not a transaction-level causal study. The underlying model rows were not queryable in the analysis environment, and the source summary does not identify currency or reporting period. Explain that scope plainly; do not claim a verified mapping defect, growth trend, or profit result.
